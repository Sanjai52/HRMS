Interpretation:
- Routed writes to a dead shard fail closed in <35ms (pg connect refused -> the
  dependencyUnavailable shim maps to 503); no partial write lands anywhere.
- Aggregate/scatter-gather reads keep serving 200 with meta.partial true and the dead
  shard named; other shards' data intact (headcount from catalog; day rows 7; team
  100 -> 150 after recovery).
- Recorded divergence: the brief's "/employees?search=" partial expectation maps to the
  implemented scatter-gather endpoint GET /api/v1/employees/team. GET /api/v1/employees
  is a catalog employee-directory read by design (no shard dependency, no meta).
- Check-in is idempotent across the outage/recovery boundary (duplicate:true, same
  attendance_id).
